## 📌 Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze customer purchasing behavior and marketing campaign responses to identify valuable customer segments, understand purchasing channels, and evaluate campaign performance.

---

## 🧩 Business Questions Addressed
* Which customer segments contribute the most spending?
* Which countries and education groups generate higher spending?
* What factors are associated with campaign response?
* Which purchasing channels are most preferred?
* How does customer spending vary across product categories?
* How does deal usage relate to customer spending?
* Which marketing campaigns receive the highest customer acceptance?

## 🎨 Dashboard Pages & Key Features

### 1. Marketing Executive Overview
A high-level tracking canvas providing a macro view of business health, geographic distribution, and customer value mapping.
* **Key Metrics Monitored:** Total Customers (2K), Total Spending (\$7M), Total Purchases (140K), Campaign Response Rate (15%), Average Spending per Customer (\$606).
* **Core Visuals:** Geographic breakdown chart, education-based spending plots, customer value distribution, and a response breakdown pie chart.

### 2. Campaign & Channel Performance
A granular assessment of marketing performance across distinct channels and targeted promotional runs.
* **Key Metrics Monitored:** Complaint Rate (1%), Total Campaigns Accepted (3,335), Total Campaign Responses (334).
* **Core Visuals:** Campaign acceptance comparison chart (Campaign 4 leading), procurement channel funnel (Store: 65K, Web: 46K, Catalog: 30K), and a deal usage spending trend line.

### 3. Customer & Product Insights
An exploratory demographic workspace focusing on purchasing behavior relative to household factors and product types.
* **Key Metrics Monitored:** Average Household Income (\$52.25K benchmark).
* **Core Visuals:** Spending trends on goods (Wine and Meat as category leaders), spending breakdowns sorted by number of children, and overall value segment contributions.

## 💡 Data-Driven Insights

* 🌍 **Geographic Powerhouse:** **Spain** represents the absolute core market, generating over **\$3M in total spending**, which significantly doubles the output of the next highest country (Saudi Arabia).
* 🎓 **Academic Demographic Trends:** Customers holding a **Graduation level degree** make up the largest spending block by education, out-indexing PhD and Master's holders combined.
* 🏬 **Fulfillment Preference:** **Store-based transactions (65K)** lead as the primary sales driver, but digital touchpoints are strong with **Web purchases tracking at 46K**.
* 🍷 **Category Champions:** **Wine spending** sits heavily as the primary revenue category driver across all tracked goods, followed strongly by Meat products.
* 👨‍👩‍👦‍👦 **Household Elasticity:** Average spending drops off dramatically based on family scale. Customers with **0 children spend nearly double (\$1,000+)** the amount of households with 1 child (~\$500), dropping even lower as child count grows.
* 📉 **Campaign Velocity:** **Campaign 4 and Campaign 3** proved to be the most widely accepted iterations, capturing the highest aggregate customer conversions.
