# 📉 Business Case Study: Optimizing Marketing ROI & Customer Lifetime Value

## 📌 1. The Context
The executive team at **Maven Marketing** has completed a major promotional rollout spanning five separate localized marketing campaigns, alongside a final conversion push. The company captures extensive transactional data across physical and digital storefronts, alongside deep demographic profiles for 2,000 active customer accounts. 

---

## ⚠️ 2. The Core Problem Statement
Despite regular promotional expenditures across Web, Store, and Catalog channels, the marketing department faces **declining conversion efficiencies and stagnating retail returns**. 

The current approach treats the customer database as a monolithic group. By blasting uniform promotional offers across the entire footprint, Maven Marketing is experiencing:
* **High Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC):** Budget is wasted targeting segments with zero historical conversion propensity.
* **Margin Erosion:** Excessive deal usage and untargeted discount codes are cutting into product profit margins without driving long-term loyalty.
* **Sub-Optimal Inventory Control:** Slower-moving inventory classes are left unpromoted, while high-demand categories (like Wine and Meat) are not being leveraged as cross-selling anchors.

---

## 🎯 3. Objective of the Analysis
The goal of this Power BI data model is to engineer an interactive intelligence canvas that enables stakeholders to segment the database dynamically. 

By analyzing behavioral historical data, this project aims to answer:
1. **Demographic Elasticity:** Which high-value customer personas (defined by income, education, and household scale) drive the core revenue volume?
2. **Channel Efficiency:** Where should marketing spend be funneled to maximize conversions (Store vs. Web vs. Catalog)?
3. **Campaign Attainment:** Which historical campaign structures (Campaign 1–5) yielded the highest conversion benchmarks to model future iterations after?

---

## 💡 4. Expected Business Outcome
Transitioning from a blanket marketing framework to a **highly granular, data-driven segmentation engine** will allow Maven Marketing to:
* 📉 Reduce overall marketing spend by cutting underperforming regional initiatives.
* 📈 Boost Campaign Response Rates past the baseline 15% mark through targeted demographic matching.
* 💰 Maximize Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) by engineering highly tailored product-bundle triggers.
